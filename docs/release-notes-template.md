## Install

1. Download `mk8dx.apworld` below and put it in your Archipelago install's
   `custom_worlds` folder (or use *Install APWorld* in the Archipelago Launcher).
2. Open the Archipelago Launcher and click **MK8DX Client**. On the first run
   the setup wizard opens by itself (later: type `/setup` in the client). Pick
   Ryujinx, your Switch's SD card, or a folder, and click **Install**. The
   Switch mod ships ready-made inside the apworld: nothing to compile, no
   game files to extract, and it works on any network.
3. Start Mario Kart 8 Deluxe (update **4.0.0**) and connect the client to your room.

Guides: [first-time setup](https://github.com/mdietz94/mk8dx_ap/blob/main/docs/first-time-setup.md),
[Switch install](https://github.com/mdietz94/mk8dx_ap/blob/main/docs/install-switch.md),
[changing servers](https://github.com/mdietz94/mk8dx_ap/blob/main/docs/changing-servers.md).

Verify the download: `mk8dx.apworld.sha256` holds its SHA-256; the wizard also
checks the bundled mod against its own manifest before installing it.
